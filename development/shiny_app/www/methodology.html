See how a signal becomes EI.

One modal. Two ways to assign the tail. The same final EI formula.

No accepted tail peak → use s₀. Highest unused candidate wins; ties use nearest position, then smaller size. Missing step → skip.

Seed floor = max(100 RFU, 3% of modal); competing-peak dominance ≥3×. Freeze the seed posterior. Accept unique later candidates; unresolved in-scope assignments can make EI unavailable.
